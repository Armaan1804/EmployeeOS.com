Frequently Asked Questions

Our platform is free to configure. You only pay $29 per active contractor per month. If you have registered freelancers who do not submit timesheets or receive payouts during a billing cycle, you are billed $0 for those workers.

Clients can pay their consolidated contractor bills using wire transfers, ACH (U.S.), SEPA (Europe), or major credit cards. Credit card payments incur a 3.5% payment processor fee.

Freelancers receive payouts directly to their bank accounts via Stripe Connect. We support payouts in local currencies across 120+ countries, with standard bank transfers clearing in 1–2 business days.

Contractor Management Plus ($99/contractor/month) is an upgraded compliance tier. It provides automated audits on freelancer work habits and adds up to $100,000 in legal indemnity coverage. If local authorities dispute the worker status, EmployeeOS covers classification-related fines and penalties up to the limit.
