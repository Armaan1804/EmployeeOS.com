Sarah Mitchell · Employment Lawyer, UK & EU
Can I hire a full-time employee in Germany without setting up a local entity?
Yes — through EmployeeOS's Employer of Record service, you can hire in Germany immediately. We become the legal employer on your behalf, handling the employment contract under German law, payroll taxes, and social contributions. No entity setup required.
What are the notice period requirements?
In Germany, the statutory minimum is 4 weeks. This scales with tenure — up to 7 months for employees with 20+ years of service. I'd recommend including at least 3 months in the contract for senior roles.
