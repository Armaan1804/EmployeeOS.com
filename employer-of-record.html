Frequently Asked Questions

An EOR acts as the legal employer for your staff in countries where you do not have a registered legal entity. The EOR manages employment contracts, local tax withholding, pensions, health benefits, and payroll, while you manage the employee's day-to-day work tasks.

While standard industry EOR onboarding can take 30 days, EmployeeOS's average is just 2.3 days. Our direct owned entities and local experts allow us to draft, review, and execute compliant contracts in record time.

An **EOR (Employer of Record)** acts as the sole legal employer on paper, assuming all legal and compliance liabilities for the worker. A **PEO (Professional Employer Organization)** acts as a co-employer. Under a PEO, your company must already have a local legal entity in the country or state, and you share legal liabilities with the PEO provider.

We offer localized benefit packages tailored to each country. This includes mandatory statutory requirements (like local health plans or pensions) and supplementary benefits (life insurance, dental, vision, mental health budgets, and wellness stipends) at corporate-negotiated rates.

We employ a dual-contract IP protection mechanism called EmployeeOS **IP Guard**. We sign strict IP transfer agreements with the employee under local laws, which immediately assign all patents, software, designs, and code to EmployeeOS, and we legally transfer 100% of those rights back to your business.
