Frequently Asked Questions

A PEO is a co-employment arrangement where EmployeeOS shares legal employment responsibilities with your company. We manage payroll processing, SUTA tax filings, compliance updates, and benefits administration under our corporate FEIN, while you direct the employee's daily roles and milestones.

To use our PEO, you must have a registered U.S. legal entity, a U.S. corporate bank account, and be billed in USD. If you want to hire U.S. workers but do not have a U.S. entity, you should use our **Employer of Record (EOR)** service instead.

**PEO** co-employs workers under your own U.S. business registry, which means you manage direct state entity registrations and retain ultimate employment ownership. **EOR** hires employees directly through EmployeeOS's local entity, meaning you do not need to register a business entity in the worker's state or country.

Through our co-employment scale, clients gain access to comprehensive large-group medical, dental, and vision insurance pools from top-tier carriers like Aetna and Blue Cross Blue Shield. We also offer 401(k) pension plans, HSA/FSA configurations, and disability coverages at lower group premium rates.

EmployeeOS co-registers your account with state agencies, calculates required state unemployment (SUTA) and income taxes, processes local withholding payments each cycle, and handles quarterly tax reporting filings. We issue annual tax forms (W-2s) directly to your employees.
