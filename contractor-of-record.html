Frequently Asked Questions

Contractor of Record is a compliance-focused service where EmployeeOS contracts directly with your global freelancers. We handle contract generation, onboarding, payment processing, tax documentation, and compliance audits while acting as a legal shield between your company and contractor status claims.

Unlike competitors that limit their indemnity liability to $10,000 or $50,000, EmployeeOS COR offers **uncapped indemnity**. If a local labor or tax authority challenges the classification of a contractor under our program, EmployeeOS handles the entire legal defense, assumes all representation, and covers all fines and back taxes directly with zero cost to you.

An **EOR (Employer of Record)** is used to hire full-time employees in foreign countries without entities, managing full employee payroll, benefits, and local statutory rights. A **COR (Contractor of Record)** is designed for independent contractors, enabling safe engagement without employee-like schedules or liabilities, and protecting your firm from misclassification audits.

IP Guard is our proprietary intellectual property assignment framework. EOR and contract laws differ globally, and standard contracts often leave ownership gaps in local courts. IP Guard secures intellectual property assignment from the worker to EmployeeOS, and immediately transfers all titles, copyrights, and patents to your company upon payment release.

It runs in the background, analyzing working schedules, tool logins, invoice structures, and survey inputs to ensure contractors operate independently. If it detects employee-like patterns (e.g., sole income dependency, fixed hours), it alerts your compliance team and suggests adjustments to maintain safe classification.
